Heights are the ball's centre above the floor, in meters. The faint disc on the floor is the spot under the ball; at a touch it should be next to the player who touched it. Players stand where their feet are; while they jump they're lifted by their feet height. "Over the net" is how far the ball clears the tape where it crosses. Uncertainty is the fit's typical 1-sigma position error for that flight, mostly along the phone's line of sight.
Physics: gravity plus air drag (a volleyball slows noticeably in flight). Flights with few 2D points or a large uncertainty are marked unsure and drawn dashed: don't read exact numbers off them. Touches the 2D tracker missed (a block, a touch behind a player) are found from where one arc becomes two. Frames with no ball in play (after the rally) stay empty. Frame numbers match the video.
